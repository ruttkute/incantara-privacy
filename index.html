Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy describes how Incantara: Witchcraft & Sigils (the app, package ID cc.visualtools.notte), developed by ruttkute, handles your personal data. We collect only what is necessary to operate the app and do not sell or share your information with advertisers.

What We Collect

Data Source Why
Name, email address, profile picture Google Sign-In Authentication — to identify your account and display your name
Subscription status and purchase history RevenueCat To verify active subscriptions and grant access to paid features
Conversation history and app settings Cloudflare KV To persist your sessions, preferences, and working history across devices
What we do not collect No location data. No device sensors. No advertising identifiers. No analytics or tracking pixels. No data is sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers, ever.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to operate the app for you:

Your Google account details are used to authenticate you and display your name inside the app. We do not use your email for marketing.

Your subscription status (managed by RevenueCat) determines which features you can access. We store only the entitlement result, not raw payment card data — payment processing happens entirely within RevenueCat and Google Play.

Your conversation and session data is stored in Cloudflare KV so the app can recall your history and preferences across sessions. This data is tied to your account and is not shared with other users.

Third-Party Services

Incantara uses the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy.

Google Sign-In (Google LLC)

Used for authentication. Provides your name, email address, and profile picture to the app after you grant permission. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

Used to manage in-app subscriptions. Receives your app user ID and purchase events from Google Play. Governed by RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Cloudflare (Workers & KV)

Our backend infrastructure. Your requests pass through Cloudflare Workers and your account data is stored in Cloudflare KV. Cloudflare may log standard network metadata (IP address, timestamps) per their infrastructure policies. Governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Groq Cloud

Used to process natural language queries inside the app. Your queries are sent to Groq's API to generate responses. Governed by Groq's Privacy Policy. We do not store query content beyond your session history in Cloudflare KV.

Data Retention

Your account data (conversation history, preferences) is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion, we will remove your data from Cloudflare KV within 30 days.

Subscription records in RevenueCat are governed by RevenueCat's retention policy. We cannot delete records held by third-party billing systems that are required for transaction verification.

Your Rights

You may request to access or delete your personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below. To delete your account data, email us with the subject line "Data deletion request" and the email address associated with your Google account.

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under the GDPR, including the right to data portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children

Incantara is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be noted by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
